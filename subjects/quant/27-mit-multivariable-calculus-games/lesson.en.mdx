## Play the MIT multivariable calculus course

<a href={quantLessonHref('QUANT-GAMES')}>← Back to the game library</a>

The games follow MIT 18.02SC Fall 2010 (Prof. Denis Auroux): vectors and matrices, partial derivatives, double and line integrals in the plane, then triple integrals and surface integrals in space. The plan is 69 games covering all 102 sessions and 5 exams; finished games appear below as they are released.

Choose any game or follow the course order. Each game has optional hints, worked reasoning, figures where the geometry matters, and its own source links. Sources open in another tab so you can read the notes, original problems and solutions without losing your answers. Numbers accept fractions such as 1/3, and exact values such as 3pi/4 or sqrt(2). There is no timer or lost life.

## How to use the materials

Try a step first, open a hint, then read the corresponding notes if the idea is unfamiliar. Use the original problems for another example and consult solutions after your own attempt. Review and exam games combine topics; their small-number questions are adaptations of the source themes, rather than reproductions of every original question.

## Continue beyond these templates

Game completion records practice, not mastery. These bounded templates do not assess sketching, proof writing, or every problem in the course PDFs, and a correct number does not by itself show that you understand the geometry. After a round, explain the final answer in your own words, solve an unfamiliar source problem and revisit the topic later without hints.

## How to use the collection

With a strong foundation, begin with representative exam or assignment problems and return to the corresponding lecture when a derivation needs review. Linear algebra and calculus support the probability, statistics and matrix-method courses.

The five MIT courses retain their complete official static ZIP packages. PDFs are also indexed individually. Audio/video files are not part of those packages and have not been downloaded; the official video links and downloaded transcripts remain available. The Aalto collection is a public 175-page lecture note with chapter exercises, not a full OCW-style exam and video package. Source language and original attribution are preserved.

## Course graph and source locations

The graph adds course hubs and topics drawn from official resource titles and PDF chapter bookmarks. Existing concepts are linked when their names or reviewed synonyms match. A course-coverage edge states that a topic is covered in that course; it does not assert a prerequisite. Each indexed topic links to its original PDF page. The original Aalto prerequisite graph remains available.

## Why optimization needs it

For vector residuals r(x), the Jacobian J describes how each parameter affects each residual. The least-squares loss L=½‖r‖² has gradient Jᵀr. Gauss–Newton approximates its Hessian by JᵀJ and solves (JᵀJ)Δ=−Jᵀr. A stage checks when that matrix cannot simply be inverted.

## Limits and further practice

Correct blanks do not establish geometric understanding, differentiability proofs or optimizer implementation skills. A fixed step size has no universal descent guarantee; Gauss–Newton has no general global convergence guarantee. Try an unfamiliar residual function next: derive J yourself and check a descent direction.

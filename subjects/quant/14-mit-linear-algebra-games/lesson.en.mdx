## Play the complete MIT course

<a href={quantLessonHref('QUANT-GAMES')}>← Back to the game library</a>

40 games follow every teaching, review and exam session in MIT 18.06SC Fall 2011, plus one optional game for Strang's 2023 farewell lecture. Start with linear equations, work through the four subspaces, projections and eigenvalues, then reach SVD, pseudoinverses and Fourier transforms.

Choose any game or follow the course order. Each game has optional hints, worked reasoning and its own source links. Sources open in another tab so you can consult the lecture notes, original problems and solutions without losing your answers. Numbers accept fractions such as 1/3. There is no timer or lost life.

## How to use the materials

Try a step first, open a hint, then read the corresponding notes if the idea is unfamiliar. Use the original problems for another example and consult solutions after your own attempt. Review and exam games combine topics; their small-number questions are adaptations of the source themes, rather than reproductions of every original question.

## Continue beyond these templates

Game completion records practice, not mastery. These bounded templates do not check every proof, arbitrary large-matrix computation or every problem in the course PDFs. After a round, explain the final answer in your own words, solve an unfamiliar source problem and revisit the topic later without hints.

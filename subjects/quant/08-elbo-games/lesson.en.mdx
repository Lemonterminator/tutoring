## Limits and source lesson

The discrete example holds the model fixed while changing q. Gaussian q has fixed variance 1, so an optimal mean does not match the full posterior. A single-sample estimate is not the exact ELBO.

Formulas follow [Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes, Section 2 and the Gaussian appendix](https://arxiv.org/html/1312.6114v11).

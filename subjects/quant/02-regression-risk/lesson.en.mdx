## Place in the learning path

Read this after probability distributions, regression and hypothesis testing. Your original material connects regression evaluation to financial risk through the same statistical objects: errors, distributions, quantiles, likelihoods and dependence.

Part I (§0–§7) derives point-error metrics, coverage, CRPS, PIT/ECE, heteroscedastic NLL and the evaluation protocol. Part II (§7.5–§13) applies these ideas to Sharpe uncertainty, multiple testing, VaR backtests, density forecasts, VaR/ES scoring, joint calibration, GARCH/QLIKE and conformal prediction.

## Reading and transfer

Begin with RMSE versus MAE, then coverage, CRPS/PIT and NLL. Continue with the finance introduction and backtest statistics before VaR or GARCH. Sections 11.5–11.6 explain the boundary between marginal and joint calibration.

The original evaluation population comes from spray experiments. Keep that context separate when transferring results to returns: temporal dependence, leakage, changing distributions and tail behavior require explicit assumptions.

## Derivations and experiments

| Chapters | Question to answer | Reading and exercise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| §1–§2 Point errors and coverage | What do bias, outliers and interval width each change? | Move the error-distribution controls and compare RMSE, MAE and coverage. |
| §3–§5 Probabilistic scores and calibration | How do calibration and predictive accuracy differ? | Compare the CRPS, PIT and NLL derivations while varying predictive location and scale. |
| §8–§10 Financial backtests | How do serial dependence and multiple testing affect conclusions? | Examine the assumptions behind Sharpe uncertainty, VaR exception tests and density tests. |
| §11–§11.6 Quantiles and joint risk | Is individual-variable calibration enough for a portfolio? | Start from pinball loss, then read the joint-calibration counterexample and Rosenblatt transform. |
| §12–§13 Volatility and conformal prediction | Why can different losses or coverage guarantees lead to different judgments? | Operate the QLIKE experiment and state the conditions required for conformal coverage. |

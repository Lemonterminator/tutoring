## 1. First, create a partial derivative table

$$
(d_1)_x=\frac{m_x}{\tau}+\left(\frac12-\frac{m}{\tau^2}\right)\tau_x,\quad(d_2)_x=(d_1)_x-\tau_x.

## 2. Vega: The CDF derivative term does not fully cancel this time

When differentiating with respect to $\sigma$, $A,B$ remains constant. By the density identity, set $H=A\phi(d_1)=B\phi(d_2)$:

$$
\nu=C_\sigma=H((d_1)_\sigma-(d_2)_\sigma)=H\tau_\sigma=A\phi(d_1)\sqrt T.
$$

Since $S,\sigma,T>0$ and $\phi>0$, Vega is positive. The same density factor can be eliminated from the Gamma formula:

$$
\nu=\sigma T S^2\Gamma.
$$

This connects "differentiation with respect to diffusion intensity" and "second-order differentiation with respect to the state variable." It applies to constant-parameter European BS Call/Put options here, and is not a universal identity for arbitrary pricing models.

## 3. Rho and dividend sensitivity

Differentiating with respect to $r$, $A_r=0$, $B_r=-TB$, and $d_{1,r}=d_{2,r}$. The CDF derivative terms cancel, leaving

$$
\rho_C=C_r=TB\Phi(d_2).
$$

Differentiating with respect to $q$, $A_q=-TA$, $B_q=0$, similarly

$$
\psi_C=C_q=-TA\Phi(d_1)=-TS\Delta_C.
$$

These derivatives depend on the convention that "other variables are held fixed." In reality, term structures, dividends, and forwards may change simultaneously; true portfolio shocks require chaining the propagation of input changes.

## 4. Theta: First determine the remaining time to maturity, then change the sign

$A_T=-qA$, $B_T=-rB$, $d_{1,T}-d_{2,T}=\tau/(2T)$. Therefore

$$
C_T=-qA\Phi(d_1)+rB\Phi(d_2)+H\frac{\tau}{2T}.
$$

As calendar time moves forward, the remaining time to maturity decreases, so $\Theta=-C_T$:

$$
\Theta_C=-\frac{A\phi(d_1)\sigma}{2\sqrt T}-rB\Phi(d_2)+qA\Phi(d_1).
$$

The first term is the diffusion/curvature contribution, the second term relates to cash discounting, and the third term relates to dividends. Theta is not negative for all parameter values; for example, the dividend term may change the sign, so one should not treat "usual time decay" as a sign theorem.

Substitute it and Delta/Gamma back into the PDE; it should satisfy

$$
\Theta+\frac12\sigma^2S^2\Gamma+(r-q)S\Delta-rV=0.
$$

**Paper pause C:** In scenario 1, $r=q=0$, use the PDE rather than the Theta closed-form expression to find Theta.

## 5. How do numerical methods uniformly handle these Greeks?

Treat the price as a function of six inputs $V(S,K,r,q,\sigma,T)$. For a selected input $x$, define the central difference and CS:

$$
V_x\approx\frac{V(x+h_x)-V(x-h_x)}{2h_x},\quad V_x\approx\frac{\operatorname{Im}V(x+ih_x)}{h_x}.
$$

Vega bump $\sigma$; Rho bump $r$; dividend sensitivity bump $q$; Theta is the negative of the derivative obtained from bump $T$. Each input has different dimensions, so a single absolute step size cannot be used uniformly.

For the central difference of $\sigma,T$, it is necessary to ensure $x-h_x>0$. One-sided formulas may be used near boundaries, but the order of accuracy must be re-analyzed. CS can mathematically avoid crossing the real axis, yet it still relies on internal complex sqrt/log and dispatching; simply forcing complex numbers into double parameters does not guarantee it will work.

For ordinary first-order partial derivatives, both central differences and complex-step using the full analytic price have truncation error $O(h_x^2)$. After the finite truncation CDF passes through the composite chain of new parameters, verification should be performed direction by direction; having verified Spot Delta does not imply that Vega/Theta are automatically verified.

## 6. Establishing three types of consistency checks

Comparing analytic formulas with numerical partial derivatives is the first type. The PDE residual $R_{PDE}$ is the second type. The Vega-Gamma relationship residual is the third type:

$$
R_\nu=\nu-\sigma TS^2\Gamma.
$$

These residuals can locate unit, sign, and formula errors, but shared code errors may simultaneously reduce multiple residuals. At least combine independent high-precision price differentiation and non-zero $r,q$ scenarios; zero interest rate and zero dividend will hide many errors.
